## Prince of Peace (painting by Akiane Kramarik)

- **Kind:** painting; related work (of heaven-is-for-real), matched by name only; presented as a true account.
- **First widely available:** 2002-07, painted, at age eight ([Official Akiane Gallery](https://akiane.com/my-story/); [CBS News Chicago, 2019-12-13](https://www.cbsnews.com/chicago/news/akiane-kramarik-prince-of-peace); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akiane)). No source gives the year. She was born in July 1994 and painted it at eight, so July 2002 is the earliest it could have existed. She appeared on Oprah Winfrey's show at eight or nine (sources differ). Only the validity check uses this date.
- **Names matched:** Prince of Peace; Akiane Kramarik; Akiane
- **Note:** The boy in Heaven Is for Real picked this painting as the Jesus he saw. "Prince of Peace" is also a biblical title (Isaiah 9:6), but names are matched only in fields that name a work, never in running text.
